## Email 2: Questions to Ask Before Approving a Major Repair

Subject: A few questions to ask before approving the repair

Hi,

Before approving a major repair, it can help to ask the shop a few plain questions:

- What problem did you find?
- How did you confirm the diagnosis?
- Is the vehicle safe to drive right now?
- What does this repair fix?
- What does it not fix?
- What warranty covers parts and labor?
- Are there related repairs likely soon?

The goal is not to challenge every mechanic. It is to understand the repair well enough to compare your options calmly.

If the quote is expensive or unclear, a written second estimate may be worth considering when the car can be inspected safely.

## Email 3: Repair Bill vs. Replacement Cost

Subject: Do not compare only the repair bill to the car's value

Hi,

A common shortcut is to compare the repair bill to the car's value. That can be useful, but it is not the whole decision.

Also compare:

- Expected repairs over the next year.
- Remaining loan balance.
- Replacement down payment.
- Monthly payment.
- Taxes, title, registration, and dealer fees.
- Insurance and fuel changes.
- Reliability needs for work, school, caregiving, or commuting.

A paid-off car with a large repair may still cost less than replacing it. But a repair that leaves you with an unsafe or unreliable car may not solve the real problem.

## Email 4: When a Second Estimate May Be Worth It

Subject: When to get a second repair estimate

Hi,

A second estimate may be worth considering when:

- The quote is expensive.
- The diagnosis is unclear.
- The repair involves the engine, transmission, hybrid battery, brakes, steering, or electrical system.
- The warranty is hard to understand.
- The car is safe enough to inspect elsewhere, or it can be towed.

If there are possible brake, steering, airbag, rust, flood, structural, or severe drivability concerns, safety should come first. Ask a qualified professional whether the vehicle should be driven.

## Email 5: After You Compare Your Options

Subject: What to do after comparing repair and replacement

Hi,

After you compare your options, the next step is usually practical:

- If repair looks reasonable, ask for the final written estimate and warranty terms.
- If replacement looks reasonable, compare realistic used and new options before shopping emotionally.
- If it is a close call, get more information before committing.
- If safety is uncertain, have the vehicle inspected before driving or deciding.
